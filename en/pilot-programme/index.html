University pilot programme

One term, one real assignment,
certificates we read together.

The pilot programme runs Certimens on a real assignment in your course — a dissertation, a placement report, a coding project — with your teachers and your students. You judge on evidence, not on a demo.

What the pilot includes

A single all-inclusive package for one term. No technical option is charged separately.

If you move on to an annual subscription within 60 days of the pilot, its price is deducted in full from the first year.

  • One term (three to four months), with no automatic renewal
  • Up to 15 teachers and 300 enrolled students, unlimited assignments
  • Launch from Moodle (LTI 1.3) or SSO sign-in, class import from CSV or Excel
  • The four open-source agents (Word, LibreOffice, Visual Studio Code, browser) and the online editor, usable offline
  • Interface and PDF certificates in French and English
  • Set-up with your IT department, a 45-minute teacher workshop and a closing review

Seven days from agreement to first assignment

We do the set-up work with you. Your teams have no server to deploy.

  1. D1

    Opening your space

    Once the agreement and purchase order are in, we create your institution's space and set the retention period you chose.

    Pilot agreement, data processing agreement (Art. 28) and DPIA template provided.

  2. D3

    IT set-up

    A video call with your administrator to declare the LTI 1.3 tool in Moodle — or enable SSO and class import — and validate a test course.

    Nothing to install on your side.

  3. D5

    Teacher workshop

    45 minutes to create an assignment with its deadline and read sample certificates together: what they say, and what they do not.

    The certificate informs the decision; it does not make it.

  4. D7

    Student launch

    Your teachers paste five ready-made lines into the brief: a one-minute start, the privacy guarantees, and the accommodation route.

    Scores stay hidden from students until the deadline.

What makes a pilot worth running

New work

Certimens cannot measure after the fact what was written before it was switched on. The pilot starts with an assignment, a report or a dissertation cycle that is just beginning.

A text of real length

A few lines draw no writing profile. We recommend work of at least 1,500 to 2,000 characters, written outside the exam room.

A team that reads the certificates

The review is done on your scripts: we go through the certificates with your teachers, case by case, including the ones that raise questions.

What we commit to

  • No keystroke and no text captured by the agents, whose code is public
  • Platform and data hosted in France, at OVHcloud, with no third-party resources
  • No automatic mark or sanction: the decision stays with the teacher
  • A measurement exemption, with no reason recorded, for students with exam accommodations
  • If the pilot ends with no follow-up: a full export of your data, then destruction certified in writing

What is measured, kept and erased is set out in our privacy policy.
