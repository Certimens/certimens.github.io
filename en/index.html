Frequently asked questions

How is Certimens different from conventional AI detectors?

Traditional detectors settle for a statistical analysis of the final text, which produces a great many false positives and creates a climate of suspicion. The Certimens label draws on cognitive psychology and behavioural analysis. We certify intellectual effort by analysing the writing process itself (pause times, typing dynamics, phases of reflection), which makes for an assessment that is both fair and scientifically grounded.

Does the analysis comply with the GDPR and respect students' privacy?

Absolutely. Regulatory compliance and data protection sit at the heart of our infrastructure. Certimens records no sensitive personal data and uses no intrusive surveillance (no webcam, no browser lockdown). Neither the text typed nor any individual keystroke is recorded: only counters aggregated over a measurement window, from which no content can be reconstructed. All traffic is encrypted, and our privacy policy details every piece of data processed, why it is processed and how long it is kept.

Is the tool difficult to integrate with our existing systems?

No. There is nothing to install on your servers: the student adds a browser extension for Google Docs, Word Online and Overleaf, an Office add-in for Word, the LibreOffice Writer extension, or the Visual Studio Code extension for an assignment handed in as code — which measures the project file by file. Each installs from its vendor's own store, and the Plugins page gathers all four, with the steps for each and a manual install for a managed fleet.

The student has no server address to type in, and no agent ever asks for their password: the agent shows a short code, the student confirms it in their Certimens space, and that is all. You can also point the engine at your institution's own writing spaces — its Moodle, its wiki: the extension then offers them to your students, who remain the only ones able to grant the permission, site by site. Measurements are sent in the background and queued if the connection drops, without ever asking anything of the student.

And for a first assignment, or a class where nothing can be installed, there is something simpler still: the Certimens workspace carries an editor of its own. The student writes in it, the document is built in Word format in their browser, and the measuring happens there — by exactly the same rules, so that a paper written this way is compared with one written in Word on equal terms. Nothing to install, nothing to authorise, and the document leaves only when the student saves it.

What does the “Label of Excellence” add to our degrees?

Now that generative AI has made content production trivial, guaranteeing the authenticity of your students' work is becoming a decisive competitive advantage. Displaying the Certimens Label of Excellence on your degrees and dissertations reassures recruiters and partner companies about your graduates' genuine cognitive and analytical skills, and strengthens the standing of your institution.

Can anyone check what the agent actually measures?

Yes, and that is deliberate. The agents are the only part of Certimens that runs on the student's own machine, and the only one whose code is public and free software, under the AGPL-3.0: anyone — a student, a data protection officer, your IT department — can read in it what is counted, what is sent and what is not, at github.com/Certimens/plugins. A promise that cannot be checked is not a guarantee.

Faced with the urgency of AI, we chose the human

Our scientific manifesto

Certimens was not born of a wish to police students, but of a long reflection on the science of education and on cognitive psychology. Faced with the limits of traditional AI detectors, which get it wrong and break trust, we came to the conclusion that the paradigm had to change.

By drawing on the study of how thought is formed and on behavioural dynamics (pause times, the construction of an argument), we built a certification that rewards genuine intellectual effort. Putting human cognition back at the centre of assessment: that is our mission.
