Certimens is a shield for your writing. Without watching you or recording your text, it demonstrates that your effort was genuine, so that nobody has to doubt that your work is your own.

Three guarantees for writing with a free mind

A tool that measures writing is a frightening idea. Here is exactly what it does, and what it will never do.

No spying

No webcam, no microphone, no browser lockdown. Certimens does not analyse what you write, but the dynamics of your writing effort. You stay free to pause, to delete and to work at your own pace: that is precisely what marks out human work.

Your writing stays yours

No text typed and no keystroke is ever recorded: only counters, from which no content can be reconstructed. Your writing trains no AI and is sold to nobody. A document leaves your machine only when you submit it.

The end of false accusations

AI detectors get it wrong, and it is the honest student who pays for it. By attesting that you were there at your keyboard, Certimens gives you something to defend your work with — and you can read your certificate, not just be subjected to it.

What is never measured

The list matters as much as the measurement. None of the following is read, counted or sent.

  • The text you type, and the keys you press
  • Your webcam, your microphone, your screen
  • Your other windows, other tabs and other applications
  • What you write elsewhere: measurement applies to the assignment document only
  • Your password — no agent will ever ask for it; you confirm a short code in your own space instead

And you do not have to take our word for it. The agents are the only part of Certimens that runs on your machine, and their code is public and free, under the AGPL-3.0 licence: you can read there, line by line, what is counted and what is sent.

The way you work is not on trial

Hesitating, crossing out, backtracking or losing your connection: none of it counts against you. These are the ordinary traces of human writing.

  • Your live corrections and your spellchecker: lexical micro-hesitations are among the markers of authentic writing
  • Your second thoughts and cuts: restructuring a paragraph, deleting a page, starting over — macro-editing is measured as a sign of work, not as a flaw
  • A network outage: the counters wait for the connection to come back, and nothing is lost in the meantime
  • Your pace: writing slowly, in short sittings or in one go carries no penalty — the score is weighted by the typing time of each sitting

Text you paste — a quotation, a passage from your own draft — is not an offence: it is reported separately, within the share of the document actually typed. Certimens measures, it does not judge: the interpretation is your marker's, and you read the same report they do.
