Teachers & markers

Assess the thinking.
We take care of the proof.

Certimens replaces neither your judgement nor your teaching freedom. It does the technical work — measuring, dating, documenting — so you can get back to yours: reading, understanding and marking.

Your independence, preserved — no algorithmic tribunal

Certimens imposes no verdict and does not stand in for you. It gives you facts about the context of creation — time spent, bursts of typing, hesitation, rewriting — and a report that explains them. The final assessment and the mark will always belong to your teaching expertise alone.

Zero friction — the certificate comes to you

Forget the chore of downloading, importing or dragging files into a third-party tool. The moment the student submits their work, the certificate is attached to the paper. The proof arrives with the work, without a single extra click.

The end of the "burden of proof"

Doubt costs time, and a false positive costs trust. Neither one is your job.

No detector to open

No export, no copy-paste, no second tool to learn. The certificate is already there when you open the paper.

Facts, not a percentage

We analyse not the text but the rhythm of its creation. Where a detector shows a probability, Certimens describes a process — and says what it is based on, with a confidence index stating what the sensor was actually able to see in the editor used.

An instrument, not a judge

The report ends on a writing profile, never on a sanction. You decide what to do with it, and the student can read it too.

What you read on a paper

The certificate fits on one screen. It is dated, it exports to PDF, and it stays readable years after the viva.

  • The Certimens score, computed session by session then weighted by the typing time each one carries
  • Its breakdown by dimension: rhythm, rewriting, hesitation, movement through the text
  • How much of the document was actually typed, and the shape of whatever arrived another way
  • The measurement confidence index: what the sensor was able to see, depending on whether the script was written in Google Docs, LibreOffice, Visual Studio Code or Word
  • A writing profile: authentic, copyist, hybrid — or undetermined, where the markers form no pattern
  • An expert report in plain words, saying why the profile is the one it is

The report stays confidential until the assignment deadline, to preserve the integrity of the assessment before opening the pedagogical conversation.
